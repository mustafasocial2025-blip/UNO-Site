## The brief
- Company: UNO Software & AI Company. Slogan: Empowering Innovation.
- Website background: approved deep luminous blue with a halo, orbital lines, and subtle perspective grid.
- Current focus: a lower-page founder section, not the homepage hero.
- Reference: `reference/aivora-ceos-layout.png`. It is a composition reference: giant type, almost wall-to-wall, tightly stacked, with a small metallic/glass portrait card partly overlapping the text. Do not copy the Aivora text, people, or black palette.
- Founder text: `THE MIND` / `BEHIND` / `UNO` on three lines. Center it. The words must be very large and directly below one another without the earlier equal-height grid-row gaps. No small "MEET MUSTAFA" line. Avoid large empty areas above, below, or between the words. The card sits near UNO without hiding all the lettering.
- Card: use `dist/mustafa-card.png`, showing the approved cartoon robotic Pharaoh character and the name `Mustafa` with `FOUNDER` on the same silver card. Do not replace him with a photograph or a realistic face, add a second glass layer, expose hair above the headdress, or create a new robot now. The standalone approved character is in `assets/approved-pharaoh-character.png`.
- Logo direction: blue/navy, luminous blue, O as target, slim metallic dart with cyan angular fins, stronger violet for `Software & AI Company` when on the logo. `assets/blue-logo-concept.png` is a visual concept, not a vector master or final production export.
